# Decisions

- **Direction:** an editorial, cream-and-shutter-blue page rather than a full-screen resort slideshow. Large Cormorant Garamond type, self-hosted DM Sans for practical information, asymmetrical rectangular photographs and the supplied mark. These are builder choices under the brief, not existing client brand guidelines.
- **Hotel before island:** the house and dusk pool introduce the property; the village appears only with directions. All nine supplied photographs are used, from their exact URLs with responsive width parameters. No replacement, stock search, generated art or invented sea-room photograph.
- **Bookings first:** a persistent header booking link, plus the requested opening buttons, room prices in the page, and repeated booking links at decision points. Links use the supplied booking URL and open a new tab with `noopener noreferrer`.
- **Offer placement:** after the room descriptions and rates, so guests understand the hotel before considering the discount. No extra urgency or scarcity language.
- **Room detail:** no invented descriptions or expanded amenities. The shared room/bath photographs are separate from the two room descriptions, since no sea-room image was supplied.
- **Copy:** the supplied copy is unchanged apart from layout/whitespace. Added words are navigation, field labels, accessible labels and honest form feedback, not marketing claims. The placeholder Instagram handle is deliberately not promoted.
- **Waitlist:** `.env.example` and `lib/waitlist.mjs` contain `{{WAITLIST_ENDPOINT}}`. Set the server-only Vercel variable `WAITLIST_ENDPOINT` to the real HTTPS endpoint and redeploy. `/api/waitlist` validates then POSTs JSON containing `email` and optional `name`; confirm JSON acceptance at handover because the brief specifies fields, not encoding. Without configuration it returns HTTP 503 and explicitly says nothing was saved. A success message requires upstream HTTP 2xx. No local email storage, logging, cookies, analytics or tracking. Native HTML form fallback works without JavaScript. Production service should supply rate limiting and mailing-list consent/unsubscribe handling.
- **Metadata:** the canonical domain is `https://ninecypresses.example` as instructed, with Hotel JSON-LD, social metadata, sitemap and robots. Preview-environment builds are noindex; production builds allow indexing. Schema contains only supplied facts, no reviews, ratings or coordinates.
- **Launch limitations:** `.example` is reserved; the booking, email, domain and phone details remain the supplied test values, not verified working services. This can be a live website preview, not a live booking/mailing business until those are replaced.
- **Harness disclosure:** built directly in Viktor's Teams environment, without Claude Code or another builder. Viktor's codebase, brand, landing-page and browser guidance, plus prior experiment/analyst context, were available. The experiment protocol was read to respect its boundaries; no sealed predictions or other builders' outputs were read in this run. Exact backend model identity is not independently verified. Do not classify this as a clean bare-model/skill-less baseline.
